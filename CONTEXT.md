# AntiLonely

An anonymous community and discussion platform designed for students to connect, converse, and share without social pressure.

## Language

**Notification**:
A discrete event record informing a user of an interaction involving their content, mentions, or account.
_Avoid_: Alert, notice, push

**Board**:
A thematic discussion space where users can browse, post, and engage in conversations.
_Avoid_: Room, channel, chatroom

**Post**:
A primary discussion entry submitted to a board.
_Avoid_: Topic, thread, article

**Reply**:
A response contributed to an existing post.
_Avoid_: Comment, feedback

**Reaction**:
An expressive lightweight feedback attached to a post or reply.
_Avoid_: Like, upvote
